Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At CR & SJ Bearup LTD, we are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. This policy explains how we use the information we collect about you and how you can instruct us if you prefer to limit the use of that information.

1. Information We Collect

Contact Form: When you use our email contact form, we collect your email address. This data is used solely to respond to your specific enquiry and is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies: Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience. This includes functional cookies required for:

  • Embedded Google Maps
  • Google Reviews
  • Links to our Facebook page

2. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with third-party platforms. These providers may collect your IP address and use cookies to function correctly:

  • Google: Used for Maps and Reviews. You can view Google's privacy policy on their website.
  • Facebook: Our site links to our official Facebook page for updates and social engagement.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can choose to accept or decline cookies using our website banner. Most web browsers also allow you to control cookies through their settings. Please note that declining cookies may affect the functionality of features like Google Maps and Google Reviews.

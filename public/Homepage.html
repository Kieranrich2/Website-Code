About Us:

CR & SJ Bearup Ltd is a family owned and run Farming and Agricultural Contracting Company based in Kent, incorporated since 24th April 2007.

We offer agricultural contracting services to local farmers and land owners as well as undertaking work on behalf of local Parish Councils.

We produce our own hay and haylage from approx 70 Ha of land within the local area. We also have arrangements with local grain farmers to buy and bale the straw off their fields once the crop has been combined. The hay, haylage and straw is barn stored and delivered to local farmers, farm shops, equestrian owners and centres throughout the year.
